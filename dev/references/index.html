References

Literature underpinning CausalDynamics.jl. BibTeX keys match the CDCS book references.bib where possible. For estimation (LMTP, mediation TMLE, Super Learner), see also CausalTargeted.jl references.

Adjustment-set algorithms (CausalInference / gensearch)

CausalDynamics delegates backdoor and frontdoor adjustment to CausalInference.jl gensearch. Literature underpinning those algorithms:

  • Shpitser, I., VanderWeele, T., & Robins, J. M. (2010). On the validity of covariate adjustment for estimating causal effects. In Proceedings of the Twenty-Sixth Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI). — key shpitser2010validity (arXiv:1203.3515)

  • van der Zander, B., Liśkiewicz, M., & Textor, J. (2019). Separators and adjustment sets in causal graphs: complete criteria and an algorithmic framework. Artificial Intelligence, 270, 1–40. doi:10.1016/j.artint.2018.12.006 — key vanderzander2019separators (arXiv:1803.00116)

  • Wienöbst, M., van der Zander, B., & Liśkiewicz, M. (2024). Linear-time algorithms for front-door adjustment in causal graphs. In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence. doi:10.1609/aaai.v38i18.30043 — key wienoebst2024lineartimefrontdoor (arXiv:2211.16468)

Shared BibTeX keys also appear in the CDCS book references.bib.

  • Spirtes, P., Glymour, C., & Scheines, R. (2000). Causation, Prediction, and Search (2nd ed.). MIT Press, Cambridge, MA. — key spirtes2000causation

  • Gnecco, N., Peters, J., Engelke, S., & Pfister, N. (2026). Boosted control functions: distribution generalization and invariance in confounded models. Journal of Machine Learning Research, 27(46), 1–57. https://jmlr.org/papers/v27/24-2207.html — key gnecco2026bcf

g-methods and longitudinal confounding

Time-indexed and dynamical models

Unrolled lag DAGs (TemporalDAGSpec, unroll_temporal_dag, TemporalEffectQuery) apply standard backdoor criteria on an expanded static graph, while continuous-time and kinetic extensions build on dynamical SCMs and interventional embedding entropy:

  • Pearl (2009), Ch. on dynamic models / time-indexed SCMs
  • Discrete-time CDMs in the CDCS book (Ch. 28)
  • Díaz, I., Williams, N., Hoffman, K. L., & Schenck, E. J. (2023). Nonparametric causal effects based on longitudinal modified treatment policies. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 118(542), 846–857. doi:10.1080/01621459.2021.1955691 — key diaz2023lmtp (implemented in CausalTargeted)
  • Peters, J., Bauer, S., & Pfister, N. (2022). Causal models for dynamical systems. In H. Geffner, R. Dechter, & J. Y. Halpern (Eds.), Probabilistic and Causal Inference: The Works of Judea Pearl (pp. 671–690). Association for Computing Machinery. doi:10.1145/3501714.3501752 (arXiv:2001.06208) — key peters2022causal
  • Pfister, N., Bauer, S., & Peters, J. (2019). Learning stable and predictive structures in kinetic systems. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 116(51), 25405–25411. doi:10.1073/pnas.1905688116 (arXiv:1810.11776) — key pfister2019causalkinetix
  • Shi, J., Li, Y., Zhao, J., Leng, S., Bao, R., Aihara, K., Chen, L., & Lin, W. (2026). Deciphering interventional dynamical causality from non-intervention complex systems. The Innovation, 7(10), 101358. doi:10.1016/j.xinn.2026.101358 (arXiv:2407.01621) — key shi2026interventional
  • Shi, J., Chen, L., & Aihara, K. (2022). Embedding entropy: a nonlinear measure of dynamical causality. Journal of the Royal Society Interface, 19(188), 20210766. doi:10.1098/rsif.2021.0766 — key shi2022embedding

  • Robins, J. M., & Greenland, S. (1992). Identifiability and exchangeability for direct and indirect effects. Epidemiology, 3(2), 143–155.

  • Avin, C., Shpitser, I., & Pearl, J. (2005). Identifiability of path-specific effects. In Proceedings of the 19th International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI), pp. 357–363. — key avin2005identifiability

  • VanderWeele, T. J. (2015). Explanation in Causal Inference: Methods for Mediation and Interaction. Oxford University Press, Oxford. — key vanderweele2015explanation

Discovery (optional Associations.jl bridge)

  • Spirtes et al. (2000) — PC and constraint-based search
  • Chickering, D. M. (2002). Optimal structure identification with greedy search. Journal of Machine Learning Research, 3, 507–554. — key chickering2002optimal
  • Runge, J., Bathiany, S., Bollt, E., et al. (2019). Detecting and quantifying causal associations in large nonlinear time series datasets. Science Advances, 5(11), eaau4996. doi:10.1126/sciadv.aau4996 — key runge2019detecting

Discovery outputs feed sensitivity comparisons in CausalTargeted (discovery_adjustment_sensitivity); they must not silently replace a user DAG.
